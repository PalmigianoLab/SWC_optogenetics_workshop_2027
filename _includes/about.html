About

Optogenetics lets us write activity directly into a circuit. Making sense of what comes back is harder: the same perturbation can be read as evidence for very different mechanisms, and theory and experiment often reach for different vocabulary to describe it. This workshop brings together the people doing targeted manipulations and the people modelling them, across worms, flies, fish, mice and primates.

  • Three days of talks from keynote and invited speakers, with long breaks and unstructured time by design.
  • A review article on the state of the art is the intended output, drafted collectively during the meeting.
